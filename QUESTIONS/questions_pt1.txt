ID:53 | Q:Q24. Beta-lactamase production is a mechanism of resistance among strains of Haemophilus influenzae and Neisseria gonorrhoeae. Which of the following drugs is effective against the above organisms?
OPT:a) Amoxicillin | b) Ceftriaxone | c) Clindamycin | d) Ticarcillin
ANS:B | Answer: B) Ceftriaxone
RAT:Rationale: Ceftriaxone is a third-generation cephalosporin that is stable to many beta-lactamases and is effective against beta-lactamase-producing H. influenzae and N. gonorrhoeae. Amoxicillin and ticarcillin are readily hydrolysed by beta-lactamases. (Ref: Pharmacology - antimicrobials; MOH Kenya STI guidelines.)
ID:54 | Q:Q25. Regarding warfarin:
OPT:a) It is a rapid-acting anticoagulant | b) It has poor absorption across membranes | c) It has teratogenic effects when administered in pregnancy | d) It is administered through the intravenous route
ANS:C | Answer: C) It has teratogenic effects when administered in pregnancy
RAT:Rationale: Warfarin is teratogenic (causes fetal warfarin syndrome) and is contraindicated in pregnancy. It is a SLOW-acting ORAL anticoagulant (heparin is the rapid-acting/parenteral agent) and is well absorbed. (Ref: Pharmacology - anticoagulants.)
ID:55 | Q:Q26. Cromolyn is useful in asthma because it:
OPT:a) Suppresses the release of histamine | b) Blocks adenosine receptors in the smooth muscles of bronchioles | c) Decreases bronchial hyperreactivity directly | d) Decreases bacterial load in the bronchioles
ANS:A | Answer: A) Suppresses the release of histamine
RAT:Rationale: Cromolyn (sodium cromoglicate) is a mast-cell stabiliser; it prevents the release of histamine and other mediators from mast cells, and is used for prophylaxis (not relief) of asthma. (Ref: Pharmacology - anti-asthmatic drugs.)
