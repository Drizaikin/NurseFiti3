## Critical Issues
*(Must fix before deployment — factual errors or spec violations that will reach students)*

### 1. Polyhydramnios threshold inconsistency — contradictory answers within the same cadre

Two question variants exist in the same file set for BScN Paper I that give conflicting diagnostic thresholds:

- **Batch 1:** "In polyhydromnios, the quantity of amniotic fluid exceeds:" → keyed **D = 1,500 ml**
- **Later batches:** "Clinical diagnosis of polyhydramnios is based on excessive amount of amniotic fluid exceeding" → keyed **B or D = 2,500 ml**

These are different stem texts so both clear the `ON CONFLICT` constraint and will both be inserted. A student who encounters one question and then the other will receive contradictory teaching.

The clinical position is:
- Normal amniotic fluid volume at term: ~500–1,500 ml
- **1,500 ml is the upper limit of normal, not a diagnostic threshold for polyhydramnios**
- Polyhydramnios is clinically defined as fluid volume exceeding ~2,000 ml (WHO/Myles), or AFI > 25 cm on ultrasound
- Some Kenyan midwifery curricula use 1,500 ml as a simplified cut-off — if this is the intended teaching value for NCK, the 2,500 ml variants must be removed or corrected to maintain internal consistency

**Action required:** Decide on a single threshold (recommend 2,000 ml or the curriculum-authoritative value), update all affected rows to the same answer, and remove or deprecate the contradictory variant.

---

### 2. Immunology questions mis-assigned to Paper I via wrong unit

Immunology questions (BScN only) are filed as:
```sql
paper = 'Paper I', unit = 'Community Health Nursing', topic = 'Immunology'
```

Per §12, `Community Health Nursing` is a **Paper II** unit for BScN. Filing these rows under `unit = 'Community Health Nursing'` with `paper = 'Paper I'` creates an internal contradiction: the `unit` value disagrees with the `paper` value, and any future query that derives paper assignment from unit will misroute these questions.

Immunology has no dedicated unit in the BScN Paper I list. The correct resolution is one of:
- Assign `unit = 'Medical-Surgical Nursing'` with `paper = 'Paper I'` (immunology as applied pathophysiology) and `topic = 'Immunology'`
- Or assign `unit = 'Community Health Nursing'` with `paper = 'Paper II'` if immunology is intended to sit in the community/public health context

**Action required:** Align `paper` and `unit` values. Do not leave a Paper I row with a Paper II unit name.

### 4. "Direct cause of maternal mortality" — malaria incorrectly classified

One question asks "A direct cause of maternal mortality includes:" with the keyed answer including **malaria, eclampsia, and sepsis**.

Malaria is an **indirect** maternal death cause (a pre-existing or intercurrent disease aggravated by pregnancy), not a direct obstetric cause. The WHO/ICD definition of direct maternal death is restricted to conditions arising from obstetric complications of the pregnant state: haemorrhage, eclampsia/hypertensive disease, obstructed labour, sepsis/puerperal infection, and complications of unsafe abortion.

Check whether this question was fully removed in both BScN and KRCHN batches. If a version remains with malaria in the answer, the question must either be corrected (answer re-keyed to exclude malaria) or removed.

**Action required:** Confirm removal across all cadre/paper combinations. If any instance remains, re-key the answer to a set of direct causes only.

---

### 5. Bipartite placenta listed as a cause of placenta praevia

One question asserts that bipartite placenta and succenturiate lobe are causes of placenta praevia. This is clinically questionable:

- **Succenturiate lobe** is associated with retained placental fragments and velamentous cord insertion, not a recognised direct cause of placenta praevia
- **Bipartite placenta** is not a standard aetiological factor for placenta praevia in Myles, Mayes, or WHO teaching materials

If this reflects a specific Kenyan curriculum teaching point, it should be flagged in the rationale. If it does not, the question should be corrected or removed.

**Action required:** Verify against the NCK curriculum source. If unsupported, remove or correct the answer and rationale.

### 6. Near-duplicate morning sickness question — same knowledge tested twice per cadre

Two stems for morning sickness gestation weeks are present in both BScN and KRCHN:

## Minor Issues
*(Low clinical impact — cosmetic, phrasing, or edge-case ambiguity)*

### 7. Ovarian cycle phase ordering acknowledged but not corrected in rationale

The ovarian cycle question keys answer C (Follicular, luteal and Ovulation) with a rationale that notes the internal ordering error but defends C as the only option containing all three phase names. The rationale should explicitly state the correct sequence (follicular → ovulatory → luteal) rather than leaving the student to infer it. No re-keying needed, but the rationale text needs a one-sentence correction.

### 8. "Lochia" question phrasing is ambiguous

"The lochia discharge from uterus during puerperium is" — keyed D (lochia rubra). The question phrasing could be read as asking for the general term ("lochia") or the first-occurring type. Lochia rubra as the answer is defensible clinically, but the stem should be revised to make clear it is asking for the first type of lochia, eliminating the ambiguity.

### 9. "Degree corresponds to amount of bleeding" in placenta praevia

Keyed answer B. The clinical relationship between praevia degree and bleeding amount is not reliably linear — a posterior grade IV praevia may bleed less than a grade I in some presentations. This is a common NCK teaching simplification. If retained, the rationale should note it reflects the standard teaching model rather than a strict physiological rule.

### 10. OCR artifact in monozygotic twinning question stem

The stem "In monozygotic twinning, splitting after twelfth (12th) day of fertilization is highly associated with 17" contains a trailing "17" (question number from the source document). This appears in at least one batch. The `ON CONFLICT` behaviour means only one version per cadre will survive insertion, but the surviving version may be the one with the artifact rather than the clean version — depending on insertion order. Verify which version wins and ensure the clean stem is the one retained.
